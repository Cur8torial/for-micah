# Remembering Micah Bielert

Micah Bielert, June 28, 1986 – March 8, 2025. A memorial page kept by Rae (his cousin) for the family, and his book of social posts and poetry, *I've Gripped the Phantom*, which Rae put together in Canva.

Live at https://micah-bielert.netlify.app (Netlify project `micah-bielert`, linked to `main`).

- `site/` is the published site and nothing else:
  - `index.html` lays out the page: his portrait and name, then four tabs, one showing at a time (the address says which, e.g. `#gallery`), as Rae named them: **His life** (a timeline of the eras and moments from his Keeper memorial, each with its photos and videos), **Words and lyrics** (the book, set right into the page, and his Music and Lyrics playlist), the **Gallery**, and the **Guestbook** (every photo: those added by anyone, newest first, then the ones from his Keeper memorial; anyone can add up to 20 at once). The eras in His life show their photos as sideways filmstrips. The foot of the page has Get help or make a suggestion, an email to Rae (`help` in `content.js`). `content.js` holds everything about Micah it shows. To change the words, edit `content.js`. The family's tributes are kept exactly as they wrote them.
  - `photos/` are the photos from his Keeper memorial, re-saved without any hidden metadata; `videos/` are its four videos (the three Portraits slideshows and Father and Son).
  - `book/` is the book reader: `index.html`, `pages/` (one image per page), `thumbs/` (the All pages grid), `pages.json` (each page's words, for screen readers), and `book.pdf` (the full export, for Save the PDF). Old links like `/#44` are sent on to `/book/#44`.
- Also on the page: anyone can name the people in a photo (tap a photo, then Who is here?), and the Gallery filters to photos of anyone named (`micah_photo_tags`, append-only like Memom's tags). A photo added with a year ("About when?") also joins its era in His life (1986–2004, the 90's and Florida; 2005–2026, Return to Canada; set by `era` in `content.js`). Play as a slideshow runs the Gallery full screen. On June 28 and March 8 the opening shows a line from `days` in `content.js`. Save everything downloads one zip of every photo, the guestbook and his book.
- Email alerts: each new guestbook entry and each batch of photos also goes to a Netlify form, `micah-alerts`, and Netlify emails it. The recipients are set in Netlify, not here: Project `micah-bielert`, Forms, Form notifications, Add notification, Email notification, form `micah-alerts`.
- `card/` is a printable sheet of four cards with his photo and a QR code to this page (`card/qr.png`, checked to scan).
- To keep a copy in this repo, run `python3 scripts/export_micah.py` and commit `archive/`.
- The gallery: photos people add are rows in `micah_gallery` (same project, same rules as the guestbook below: read and add only; hide to take one down: `update public.micah_gallery set hidden = true where id = '<id>';`). Photos added with a guestbook entry show in the gallery too.
- The guestbook: the three tributes from his Keeper page (Hannah, Donna, Rae) are in `content.js` and always come first; everyone else's entries are rows in the `micah_guestbook` table of the Supabase project `for-memom` (ref `ckoysoolddkpvipiwadi`), which the free plan's two-project limit made the practical home. Anyone with the link can read and sign, with a photo if they like (saved under `micah/` in the `photos` bucket, re-encoded in the browser so no location data is sent); nobody can edit or delete through the site. To take an entry down, in Supabase's SQL editor: `update public.micah_guestbook set hidden = true where id = '<id>';`. The key in `content.js` is the public one and is meant to be there.
- Links: `/book/#44` opens page 44 of the book; `#story`, `#book`, `#guestbook` and `#loved` open those parts of the page.
- The site asks search engines not to list it (`<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` in `index.html`). Remove that line to let people find it by searching.
- No build step, no trackers, no accounts. Keep this repo private.
